# Privacy notice (Public Beta)

KaikoLibrary is a local-first Windows reader and collection manager. **No account is required.** After you choose a library folder, the app reads supported image folders and ZIP files on your computer. It does not require uploading your collection and has no designed active telemetry or cloud sync.

Library links, file-access handles, favorites, tags, groups, search history, reading progress, and settings may be stored in local WebView2 user data, normally under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KaikoLibrary\UserData`. Original works remain in the folders you select. Program files and user data are separate; updates and uninstall are designed to preserve user data.

If Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is missing, its Microsoft installer may contact Microsoft services; the Runtime may also update through Microsoft's mechanisms. This does not mean KaikoLibrary uploads your library. Errors or local diagnostic records can contain file paths. Remove private paths and work names before submitting a public Issue. The app does not automatically upload diagnostic material.

The app **does not guarantee anonymity, no traces, or encrypted storage**. Anyone with access to your Windows account or other local software may be able to access this data. Close KaikoLibrary before backing up both your original images and app data. Do not attach a real database, private manga, or adult works to a public Issue.

This notice covers v1.3.0-beta.1. Feedback: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/k-kaiko/KaikoLibrary/issues).
